About Codehelp

CodeHelp is an Indian ed-tech platform founded by Love Babbar, providing comprehensive online courses, interactive labs, and resources for coding, Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), and web development for aspiring software engineers, featuring live/recorded lectures, doubt support, and challenges to boost career prep. They're known for practical skills training, featuring content on LinkedIn and YouTube, with a strong focus on placement preparation and industry-relevant skills.
